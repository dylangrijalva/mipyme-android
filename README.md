Mipyme is a complete point-of-sale (POS) system that brings together products, inventory, payments, sales, and technology in one simple solution for retailers.

It's designed for businesses that need the essentials to run their day-to-day operations without the cost, complexity, or learning curve of traditional POS systems.

## What makes Mipyme special?

Mipyme is built for entrepreneurs and small businesses that don't have the time or budget to adopt complex software.

Many business applications are packed with features that merchants rarely use. They end up paying for functionality they don't need while spending unnecessary time learning how to use it.

Mipyme takes a different approach: **keep the experience simple, provide the features that matter, and let merchants choose the capabilities they actually need.**

### 1. Easy to use

As its name suggests, Mipyme is designed to be easy to use—even for non-technical users.

You shouldn't need hours of tutorials or pages of documentation just to start selling. The application guides you through the essentials and makes common operations intuitive from the start.

The goal is simple: spend less time learning software and more time running your business.

### 2. Offline-first

<p align="center">
  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/readme/illustration-offline-dark.svg">
    <img src="assets/readme/illustration-offline-light.svg" alt="A merchant completing a sale on their phone while receipts wait to sync to a disconnected cloud" width="360">
  </picture>
</p>

Many POS systems depend heavily on a stable internet connection. Mipyme is built with an offline-first approach so that an unreliable connection doesn't have to stop your business.

You can continue using the app and processing sales even when your internet connection is unavailable or unstable, without compromising data integrity.

Once connectivity is restored, changes made offline are automatically synchronized with your account in batches.

### 3. Start with little or no upfront cost

<p align="center">
  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/readme/illustration-trial-dark.svg">

Getting started with Mipyme is simple: create an account and begin a **14-day free trial**. No credit card is required.

Once you've decided that Mipyme fits your business, you can subscribe to one of three plans: `Plus`, `Pro`, or `Business`.

Each plan is designed around different business needs, so you can pay for the features that are actually useful to you.

## Features

  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/readme/illustration-stock-dark.svg">

- **Retail POS**: Manage your day-to-day sales from a single system. Mipyme brings together product management, sales processing, inventory tracking, and other essential retail operations.

- **Inventory Management**: Keep track of your stock and know when products are running low. Mipyme helps you stay ahead of out-of-stock situations so you don't miss a sale.

- **Offline Mode**: Keep selling even without a stable internet connection. Offline functionality is available in the mobile application. Sales and other changes made offline are automatically synchronized with your account when your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or cellular data.

- **Expense Management**: Track the money going in and out of your business from one place. Mipyme helps you organize expenses and maintain a clearer picture of your business's finances.

- **Reports & Analytics**: Understand how your business is performing with clear, easy-to-read reports. Track key metrics such as revenue, profit, top-selling products, and your most loyal customers.
